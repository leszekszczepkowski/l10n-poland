Since 11 July 2025 every developer in Poland has to publish the offer prices of
flats and single-family houses on the national open data portal dane.gov.pl, at
least once a day (art. 19b of the Act of 20 May 2021 on the protection of the
rights of the purchaser of a residential unit or a single-family house and the
Developer Guarantee Fund, added by the Act of 21 May 2025, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 758).
Since 11 November 2026 the data has to follow the structure set by the Minister
of Digital Affairs (amendment Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1077): 57 fields (groups A-J),
collected by the portal from a harvester XML file published by the developer.
